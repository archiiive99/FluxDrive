Closed-loop performance

Models see the cars. Plans still hit them.

FluxDrive plans over a cost map, so every detected car enters the planner's objective.

Method overview

Cameras and a command in, a 3 s trajectory out. Everything in between lives on one 40 × 80 grid.

  1. BEV feature

    Each cell gets a feature, an occupancy and a velocity. No ego status.

  2. Flux forecast

    Occupancy moves across cell faces, so a car keeps its area.

  3. Cost map

    A cell costs the overlap of the ego footprint with the forecast.

  4. Flux Value Iteration

    The ego's probability flows across the same faces, summing every path.

  5. Training

    MaxEnt IRL trains the cost; a safety loss adds the margin.

Flux, like water

Split space into cells. A flux is how much crosses the face between two cells in one step: the upwind cell's amount times the speed across the face. What cell i loses, cell j gains, so nothing is created or lost.
